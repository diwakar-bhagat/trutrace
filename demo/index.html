TruTrace

Verified answers from seller calls. Every field carries its evidence, or it is not asserted.

The failing case. One florist, asked for a dozen red roses with delivery today. The seller hedges three times. The baseline asserts all three hedges as facts; TruTrace demotes them and keeps the proof attached.
