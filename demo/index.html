Gold labels · the human review, live

The labels behind the scores are being reviewed by a person.

Every benchmark number on this page is scored against gold labels an AI drafted during the build (gold-v1-ai-drafted). Drafted labels can bootstrap a dataset; they cannot validate the system on their own. The human review walks all 224 fields against their transcripts, field by field, and is stored on the backend as a new version, gold-v2-human-reviewed. The AI drafted labels are never overwritten.

One call · campaign_01 · the failing case

One florist, asked for a dozen red roses with delivery today. The seller hedges three times. Select a field to see the exact line it rests on.

[00:00] Agent: Hi, I am calling about red roses. Do you have a dozen available for delivery today?
[00:07] Seller: Uh, red roses, a dozen is around $35, I think.
[00:13] Seller: They are usually in stock, we get them most mornings.
[00:19] Seller: Delivery today, uh, probably tomorrow morning is safer, to be honest.
[00:26] Seller: You can order on our website.

Not established in the call, so reported unconfirmed rather than filled in: Quantity · Delivery · Next step.

The baseline asserts every hedge as a fact. Its prompt never asks for evidence, and nothing checks the spans it returns.

The campaign slice · proof that matches

One query to ten synthetic florist sellers: “Red roses, a dozen, delivery today.” A confirmed field is only as good as its proof: the span, the speaker and the timestamp all have to match gold.

Proof matching gold, gpt-4o-mini, campaign. The baseline returns no evidence at all, because its prompt never asks for it. The offline provider matches 30 of 41 in both configurations; its misses are fields it did not confirm, not wrong proof.

Limits, stated

  • Synthetic transcripts. No live audio. No real telephony. The campaign is ten written calls, not ten placed calls.
  • The labels are AI drafted. The gold labels were drafted by an AI coding agent during the build and are pending Diwakar Bhagat's human review. Every score on this page is measured against those labels, not validated ground truth.
  • Held out is not clean room. The split was frozen after a build round whose fixes were driven by failures seen across all 32 cases.
  • The offline row is self-consistency. Offline rules score 84 of 84 on held out with 0 of 45 unsupported, but the rules and the dataset grew up together. The model rows are the independent check.
  • Small numbers. 32 cases plus 10 campaign transcripts. Nothing here estimates accuracy on real seller calls.
