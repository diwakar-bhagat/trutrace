TruTrace, by Diwakar Bhagat. The workbench verifies a submitted extraction against its transcript; it does not extract in this path, and a verdict is only ever about the transcript provided.

Runs and review decisions are stored on the backend and survive redeploys. The verify endpoint is public and guarded: a 12,000 character transcript cap, the model restricted to gpt-4o-mini or the offline rules, and 10 runs per hour per visitor (held in memory; the counter resets when the service redeploys).
